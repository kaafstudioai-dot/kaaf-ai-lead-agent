AI agent · Live demo

An AI assistant that turns visitors into qualified leads.

Most website visitors leave without getting in touch. This assistant starts the conversation, asks the right questions, and sends the business a clear summary of every lead. It chats in English or German.

  1. 1
    Chats with the visitorOne friendly question at a time, in their language.
  2. 2
    Understands the projectNeeds, goals, features, timeline and budget.
  3. 3
    Scores and sends the leadHot, warm or cold, straight to the owner's inbox, after the visitor confirms.

Try saying

  • I run a small café and need a website with online ordering.
  • Ich brauche einen Chatbot für meinen Friseursalon.
  • I'm just looking at prices.

This is a demo. When it asks for a name and email, please use made-up ones unless you really want Kaaf Studio to contact you. The chat is processed by ElevenLabs, and the AI is Claude by Anthropic. You'll be asked to accept the terms before you start.
